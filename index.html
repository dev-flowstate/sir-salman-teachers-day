Final exam · 1 question · 1 mark

One last question, Sir. This one's for you.

For once, we set the paper.

Let G(t) be how grateful Class 26B4 is to you, t days after your first class with us. We know that

G(0) = 1  and  dG/dt = G

Find limt→∞ G(t).

dG/dt = G  ⇒  G(t) = Cet
G(0) = 1  ⇒  C = 1, so G(t) = et
et → ∞ as t → ∞
Answer: ∞. Our gratitude grows exponentially and never converges.
